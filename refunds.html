Refund Policy

LAST_UPDATED: MAY 2026

At Quizable, we are committed to providing high-quality digital learning resources. Because our masterclasses and mock test data are digitally delivered and accessible immediately upon purchase, we maintain a strict refund policy.

1. General Policy for Digital Goods

All purchases of premium mock tests, subscriptions, and masterclasses are final and non-refundable. Once a transaction is completed and the digital content is unlocked on your account, we cannot issue a refund.

2. Exceptions & Eligible Scenarios

We will issue a full refund to your original payment method only under the following specific circumstances:

  • Duplicate Charges: If a technical error causes you to be charged multiple times for the same transaction.
  • Failed Delivery: If payment is deducted from your bank but the premium content fails to unlock on your Quizable account within 24 hours.

3. Requesting a Refund

If you meet one of the exception criteria above, you must submit a refund request within 7 days of the original transaction date.

  • Email us at support@quizable.online with the subject line "Refund Request".
  • Include your registered email address, transaction ID, and proof of the double charge or technical error.
  • Approved refunds will be processed and credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
